# Kareebu Plus — Promotion & Visual Production Gaps

This implementation removes the old discovery-3d references from the primary semantic category registry and makes the Promotion Engine a first-class cross-app discovery layer. Some bundled development media is still not final production advertising art.

## Production campaign art still required

Commission/licence final creative for:

- Home: Global launch, Shops discovery, Food weekend and seasonal city campaigns.
- Shops: grocery/everyday, electronics, beauty, home and merchant-specific campaign sets.
- Groceries: fresh food, household, baby, personal care and seller campaigns.
- Pharmacy: vitamins/wellness, first aid, baby care and personal care. Avoid unsupported medical claims.
- Rides: airport, scheduled rides, events and major city destinations.
- Boda: commute, event and city campaigns using motorcycle-only imagery.
- DineOut: brunch, date night, business lunch, weekend and restaurant campaigns.
- Go Out: events, family, kids, wellness, cinema, outdoor and attractions.
- Global: marketplace, tech, fashion, beauty, home, school/office, pet, baby, price and editorial campaigns.

## Category art still requiring dedicated commissioned renders

Some categories currently use the best semantically relevant Kareebu-owned product/lifestyle asset available. Dedicated photorealistic transparent cutouts should eventually replace generic development fallbacks for:

- Pet: dogs, cats, birds, small pets, beds and grooming.
- Fashion: clothing subcategories, bags, watches, jewellery and accessories.
- Beauty: makeup, fragrance, hair tools and men's grooming.
- Baby: feeding, nursery, travel and changing.
- Electronics: gaming, cameras, smart home, chargers and power banks.
- Gifts: hampers, personalised gifts, cards and chocolates.
- Home: furniture, bedding, bathroom, storage and decor.

## Merchant assets

Use real merchant logos only when Kareebu has a cleared packaged/merchant-supplied asset. Wordmark fallbacks remain development identities and must not be presented as official brand artwork.

## Product media

Live marketplace and merchant providers should supply SKU-specific media. Development fixture images are semantic placeholders, not a replacement for the real 5+ image product gallery expected from the provider.

## Campaign truth

Fixture campaigns intentionally use discovery language such as "Explore", "Shop" and "Discover". Do not replace these with percentage-off, free-delivery or exclusive-benefit claims unless the Promotion Engine receives a verified live campaign with dates, budget, eligibility and merchant/partner status.
