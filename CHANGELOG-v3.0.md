# Kareebu+ Premium v3.0 — Realistic Marketplace & Brand Pass

## Marketplace realism
- Expanded the demo restaurant catalogue to 10 restaurants with richer cuisine, ETA, rating, offer and distance metadata.
- Added full multi-section restaurant menus with dozens of realistic demo dishes across breakfast, mains, local favourites, burgers, pizza, drinks and desserts.
- Restaurant menus now have functional add/remove quantity controls, live cart count and live totals.
- Cart now reflects the selected restaurant, supports quantity changes, restaurant notes, payment method display and working demo promotion codes (`SAVE10`, `PLUSFREE`).
- Food has local restaurant/dish search, category filtering, offer/rating/ETA filtering and a richer promotions rail.
- Expanded Shops to 12 stores across pharmacy, groceries, beauty, nutrition, electronics, home, pets and eye care.
- Shops now supports store search, category filtering, offer/free delivery/ETA/rating filtering and favourite toggles.
- Universal search now includes the expanded demo restaurant and shop catalogue.

## Ride & Boda realism
- Added Kampala-style demo navigation instructions for Ride and Boda.
- Boda and car guidance have different route summaries and instruction sequences.
- Live route geometry still comes from the configured Photon/Valhalla or Google provider; demo turn guidance is explicitly labelled as demo guidance.

## Brand system
- Approved Kareebu+ wordmark is now more prominent on the Home header.
- Added consistent branded context rails across transport, parcel, wallet, account, activity, orders and global search.
- Added branded onboarding progress rails to city, phone, OTP, profile and permissions screens, while preserving the existing full logo on splash, welcome, country and location.
- Commerce headers now carry the Kareebu+ mark and branded service context.

## QA
- Updated structural validator for v3.0.
- All TS/TSX source files pass TypeScript syntax transpilation in the artifact environment.
- No new native dependencies were added.
