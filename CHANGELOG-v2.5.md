# Kareebu+ Premium v2.5 — Brand, Onboarding & Marketplace UX

## Brand system
- Locked the Kareebu+ application shell to black, yellow and red.
- Green is reserved for success/live/verified states only.
- Merchant logos and photography may retain their own brand colours without changing app chrome.
- Unified functional typography to 28px page titles, 22px section headings, 17px card titles, 15px body, 13px supporting text and 12px captions.

## First-run journey
- New red Kareebu+ splash treatment.
- Reworked fixed-height welcome screen with Get started and Continue as guest.
- Added separate country selection for Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania.
- Added searchable city selection with country-specific cities and current-location entry.
- Phone sign-in now uses the selected country dial code.

## Home
- New Kareebu+ branded header and delivery-location context.
- Reordered intent-led service grid for Food, Rides, Boda, Groceries, Pharmacies, Stores, Send and More.
- Added black/red/yellow campaign carousel.
- Added New finds marketplace strip and Redeem and save cards.
- Preserved the interactive square map below Recent activity.

## Maps
- Kept interactive pinch/pan maps, +/- zoom and current-location controls.
- Kampala starts at a wider city-level view.
- Location picker now returns users to the screen that launched it.

## Food
- New Kareebu+ offer cards.
- Top restaurants near you section.
- Category browsing and filter chips.
- Richer vertical restaurant list with ratings, ETA, fees and offer badges.
- Simplified commerce bottom navigation to Home, Orders and Account.

## Shops & pharmacy
- New category discovery strip.
- Weekend Wellness Drop promo surface in Kareebu+ colours.
- Top pharmacy brands grid.
- Browse/filter controls and detailed merchant rows.
