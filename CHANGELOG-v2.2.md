# Kareebu+ Premium v2.2

This pass moves the customer app toward the intended **Talabat × Uber × Noon** product language while keeping Kareebu+ visually distinct.

## UI system

- Tightened the core palette around black/white, Kareebu red and Kareebu yellow.
- Added a warm commerce orange for Food, with green reserved for live/success states.
- Reduced generic card shadows and tightened radii/spacing for a denser premium feel.
- Reduced service-tile size and visual weight on Home.
- Reduced the Kareebu Black promotion to a compact 146dp campaign card.

## Home

- Boda now opens the ride flow with Boda selected; Rides opens with Economy selected.
- Popular stores are now crisp native merchant cards rather than pre-cropped logo screenshots.
- Merchant cards show rating and realistic delivery-time context.
- Food cards are slightly wider and easier to scan.

## Food

- Rebuilt discovery into a horizontal marketplace layout.
- Added Food-specific orange campaign treatment.
- Added horizontal categories, larger restaurant cards, delivery ETAs/fees and Quick Picks.
- Added direct access to Cart in the Food header.

## Shops

- Removed the cropped store-logo artwork that caused the emulator issue.
- Added native high-resolution merchant identity cards.
- Added horizontal categories, a Kareebu+ Market campaign banner and Shop by need discovery.
- Added a commerce bottom nav that moves directly between Food, Shops, Orders and Cart.

## Boda / rides

- Added a journey summary before ride selection.
- Corrected the passenger semantics after booking: the passenger no longer sees a misleading “Start trip” control.
- Added a pickup verification code and clearer live state.
- Replaced “End trip” with a customer arrival acknowledgement for prototype testing.
- Safety access remains visible during pickup and live trip states.

## Prototype boundary

The app is a local functional prototype. Live dispatch, production maps, SMS OTP, payment charging, restaurant/store inventory and server-driven trip state still require backend services.
